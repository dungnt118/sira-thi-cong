## INTERACTIONS

### Gestures
- **Swipe left/right:** Next/Previous evidence
- **Swipe up:** Show info panel
- **Swipe down:** Hide info panel
- **Pinch:** Zoom in/out
- **Double tap:** Toggle zoom
- **Swipe right on image:** Quick approve
- **Swipe left on image:** Quick reject

### Buttons
- **Skip:** Navigate to next
- **Reject:** Require feedback, then submit
- **Approve:** Submit approval
