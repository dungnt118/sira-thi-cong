Validation Rules:
- Company Name OR Full Name required (based on type)
- Phone, Email, Address required
- Tax ID required for Corporate (format: 10 digits)
- Email format validation
- Phone format validation (Vietnamese format)

Behavior:
- Type radio changes form fields (Corporate ↔ Individual)
- "Save as Draft" → status = PENDING (can edit later)
- "Save & Create Contract" → saves customer + redirects to contract form
- Contact address checkbox toggles billing address field
