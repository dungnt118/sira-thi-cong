INTERACTIONS:

Tap Member Card:
1. Navigate to member detail
2. Show full information
3. Show statistics

Tap [Chi tiết] Button:
1. Navigate to member detail
2. Same as tap card

Tap Phone Number:
1. Show call dialog
2. Option to call or copy

Tap Add Button (+):
1. Navigate to staff selection
2. Show available staff
3. Allow selection

Pull to Refresh:
1. Reload team list
2. Update statistics
3. Show refresh indicator

Swipe Member Card (optional):
1. Swipe left: Show actions
   - Edit
   - Remove
2. Swipe right: Quick call
